How to use the Proposal Builder

It turns a Caterease Special Event Order into a branded client proposal in a few minutes. It runs in your browser, so the SEO never leaves your computer.

Open the Proposal Builder

Before you start

In Caterease, print the event with the Special Event Order custom print and save it as a PDF.

Chrome or Edge gives the cleanest PDF. Safari and Firefox work too.

Steps

  1. 1
    Drop in the SEODrag the PDF onto the page, or click Choose the SEO PDF.
  2. 2
    Pick a lookWedding, Corporate or Social. The tool picks one from the event, and you can switch at any time without losing your edits.
  3. 3
    Clear "Needs attention"Items with a dark bar should be fixed before sending. The rest are things to double check. Click an item to jump to that part of the menu.
  4. 4
    Review the details and menuEvery field can be edited. Drag dishes by the ☰ handle, or use the arrows or Move to. Click a dish photo to turn it on or off.
  5. 5
    Build the proposalClick Build Proposal and look through every page.
  6. 6
    Save as PDFClick Print / Save as PDF. The bar at the top shows the settings for your browser. The file is named after the event.

Good to know

PricesEvery number comes straight from the SEO. If a price is wrong, fix it in Caterease, print a new SEO and start over.
UpchargesUpgrade prices on the SEO ("additional $8.00 per person") stay off the proposal. They're listed under Needs attention so you have them.
Choices"Select 2" shows under the section heading. Dishes joined by OR on the SEO print as "Choose one".
PhotosPhotos come from the Menu Photo Lookbook. A dish without a good match prints without a photo. A possible match stays off until you turn it on.
Hiding thingsCheck "Leave off proposal" to hide a dish, or "Leave section off" to hide a whole section. "Included (not a choice)" is for things like bread service.
Nothing is savedClosing the tab clears your work. Save the PDF before you close it.

Something look off?

This is the first version and it's still being tuned. If anything reads wrong, take a screenshot and click Something look off? at the top of the tool. It opens an email to Rosie with the event details filled in. Attach the screenshot and say what should change.
